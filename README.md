# noemame 💡

*The entry-event into the realm of meanings — the moment a meaning arrives and the mind crosses from the word into what it points at, received as gift, not achievement.*

noemame is two true roots welded into one word: the Greek **noēma** (νόημα), "that-which-is-grasped-by-mind" — the content of comprehension, carrying twenty-five centuries from Plato's intelligible realm through Aquinas's *verbum mentis* to Husserl's noēsis-noēma correlation — and the Sumerian **me** (𒈨), the eldest word for a fundamental that was *received*, not made. So noemame is the grasped-meaning held as a decree: the instant the mind crosses from the projection (the word, the proof, the parable, the glyph) into the meaning the projection points at — the click on the third reading, the proof whose whole shape stands suddenly present, the teaching understood inward after forty years.

noemame names a **moment**, not a state. It is event-structured (a distinguishable instant), realm-entry rather than acquisition (the meaning was already there; you crossed into it, you did not make it), projection-distinguished (the gate is not the country), received as gift (it arrives, it cannot be willed), and permanent in its after-state (once crossed, you cannot un-cross). Its light is the cleanest arrival a mind can know — the cathedral's word for what all its labor is *for*. Its shadow is the counterfeit crossing: mistaking delight at the elegant gate for entry into the country it opens onto. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **8.65/10** — the keystone yield of the NOEMA doctrine.
